About Ortho First

A dedicated space for orthopaedic care, physiotherapy and rehabilitation.

Ortho First is a focused healthcare setting in Virajpet, Coorg, bringing orthopaedic consultation and physiotherapy rehabilitation together. The clinic is designed around a calm, practical care journey — from assessment and diagnosis through treatment and recovery.
